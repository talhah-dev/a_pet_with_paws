Why Carry A Pet with Paws®?

Discover the story, sustainable engineering, and high-fashion craftsmanship behind our revolutionary pet travel accessories.

A Pet with Paws® challenges the boundaries of traditional pet travel by combining fashion-forward design, thoughtful functionality and sustainability.

Designed for style-conscious pet parents, our pet carriers and coordinating accessories feature innovative, eco-friendly textiles made from recycled plastic bottles, transforming post-consumer waste into durable, lightweight, high-fashion materials.

Our airline-approved pet carriers have the look and feel of a luxury designer handbag while providing the comfort, safety and practicality required for pet travel.

For retailers, A Pet with Paws® offers their customers a distinctive alternative in pet travel — stylish, functional and eco-conscious.
